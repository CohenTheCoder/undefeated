How to play in 30 seconds

  1. 🎰You get a random teamA real roster from history, like the 2007 New England Patriots.
  2. 👆Draft one playerTap the position button next to a name (like QB) and he joins your field. One pick per team.
  3. 🔁Repeat until it's fullA new team comes after every pick. Bad team? Use a reroll. You get one of each kind.
  4. 🏆Play the seasonWe simulate all 16 games. Better players win more often. Go 16-0 to win.

The letter next to each player grades his season: A star,B very good, C solid starter, D role player. The exact ratings (40 to 99) are revealed after the season.

How 16-0 works

  1. Each turn you get a random real team from one season: pro football, 1940 to 2025.
  2. Draft one player from that roster into an open slot: quarterback, running back, wide receiver, wide receiver, tight end, offensive tackle, guard or center, edge rusher, defensive tackle, linebacker, cornerback, safety. Players only fit the positions they really played, and each can be drafted once.
  3. You get three rerolls, one of each: a different team from the same season, the same franchise in a different season, or a completely new spin.
  4. When your lineup is full, we simulate a 16-game season. Better players win more often, but every game carries some luck. Then come the playoffs. Win all 16 and you're undefeated.

The daily draft gives everyone the same teams, once per day. Free play lets you pick an era and draft as often as you like. Turn on blind mode to hide even the grades and trust your memory. Switch to Decades to draft from a franchise's whole decade instead of one season: each player is rated mostly on his typical season there, with a bit of credit for his best one.

During the draft each player shows a letter grade (A to D); his exact rating, from 40 to 99, is revealed after the season. Ratings compare each player with the regulars of his own season.Browse every season and roster, exploreevery franchise, see the all-time best, or read how ratings are built.
