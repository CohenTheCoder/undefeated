How 17-0 works

  1. Each turn you get a random real team from one campaign: europe's top club competition, 1955-56 to 2025-26.
  2. Draft one player from that roster into an open slot: pick a formation (4-3-3, 4-4-2, 4-2-3-1, 4-1-2-1-2 (diamond), 3-5-2, 3-4-3, 5-3-2), then fill every position from goalkeeper to striker. Players only fit the positions they really played, and each can be drafted once.
  3. You get three rerolls, one of each: a different team from the same season, the same franchise in a different season, or a completely new spin.
  4. When your lineup is full, we simulate a 17-game campaign. Better players win more often, but every game carries some luck. Then come the playoffs. Win all 17 and you're undefeated.

The daily draft gives everyone the same teams, once per day. Free play lets you pick an era and draft as often as you like. Turn on blind mode to hide ratings and trust your memory. Switch to Decades to draft from a franchise's whole decade instead of one season: each player is rated mostly on his typical season there, with a bit of credit for his best one.

Ratings run from 40 to 99 and compare each player with the regulars of his own campaign.Browse every campaign and roster, exploreevery franchise, see the all-time best, or read how ratings are built.
