How to play

Build a team of real players from sports history, one pick at a time, then see if they can win every single game of a season. That's it. Here's the whole thing in plain English.

The goal

Each game is named after a perfect record: 82-0 in basketball, 84-0 in hockey,16-0 in football, 162-0 in baseball and 17-0 in club soccer. Draft a team good enough (and lucky enough) to go undefeated. Most drafts won't. That's what makes it fun.

A draft, step by step

  1. Open a game (say 🏀 82-0). On the left is your empty lineup drawn on a court, rink, field, diamond or pitch. Each circle is a position you need to fill.
  2. You get a random real team on the right, for example the 1995-96 Chicago Bulls, with its whole roster.
  3. Draft one player. Each player has buttons on the right of his row, one for every open position he can play (PG, SG and so on). Tap one and he jumps into that spot in your lineup. You only getone player from each team, so choose well.
  4. A new team appears. Keep going: one random team, one pick, until every position is filled.
  5. Press "Simulate". We play the whole season game by game, then the playoffs. You'll see your record, how far you went, each player's season stats and your points.

Reading the roster

  • The letter is the player's grade for that season: A star or legend,B very good, C solid starter, D role player. Grades compare him with the regulars of his own season, so stars from the 1960s and the 2020s are comparable. Each player also has an exact rating from 40 to 99; those stay hidden until your season is over, then all are revealed.
  • The grey line under the name is what he actually did that season (points, goals, ERA and so on).
  • No buttons on a row? That player doesn't fit any position you still need. Players only fit positions they really played: a center can't be your point guard.
  • Sort the list by rating (Best) or by name (A–Z), and tap a position (like PG) to see only the players at that spot. Tap it again to see everyone.

Rerolls: your get-out-of-jail cards

Stuck with a weak team, or nobody fits? Each draft gives you three rerolls, one of each kind:

  • ⇄ New team: a different team from the same season.
  • ↻ New season: the same franchise, but from a different year.
  • 🎲 Full reroll: any team from any year.

Once you've used one, it's crossed out for the rest of that draft.

Ways to play

  • Daily: everyone in the world gets the same teams in the same order, once per day. Compare your result with friends.
  • Free play: unlimited drafts. You can also limit it to certain years, like only the 1990s.
  • Seasons vs Decades: in Seasons you draft from one team in one year. In Decades you draft from a franchise's whole decade (say, the 1980s Celtics), with each player rated on his typical season there.
  • Blind mode: even the letter grades are hidden until the end. Trust your memory.
  • Soccer only: pick a formation (4-3-3, 4-4-2…) above your lineup; it decides which positions you fill.

After the season

  • Your record and how far you went in the playoffs.
  • Points: wins, playoff rounds and titles all score, and a perfect season scores big.
  • Share your result as text or a link that replays your exact season, and draft again.
  • Go undefeated and you get the celebration and a banner to save. There may be a secret or two as well.

Tips

  • Fill the positions that matter most first. In football, the quarterback counts double. In baseball, it's the starting pitcher.
  • Don't burn rerolls early. A so-so team often still has one good player at a position you need.
  • A lineup full of A's is great, but a perfect season still takes some luck. Keep drafting.

Quick answers

Do I need an account?
No. It's free, there's no sign-up, and your drafts are saved in your browser.
Why can't I pick a famous player?
Either he doesn't fit a position you still need, or you've already filled that spot.
I closed the page in the middle of a draft.
Open the same game again and you'll pick up where you left off.
Where do the stats come from?
Open, public sources. See how it works & data for the details on ratings and the simulation.
